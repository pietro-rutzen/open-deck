Audit changed scope: do NOT install a bounded channel yet. Blocking sends reach the CoreMIDI synth callback; nonblocking rejections can lose release edges and partially accept multi-command loads/effects/headphone transitions. Reserved capacity alone cannot guarantee releases. This task prepares safe grouping and measures queue demand; upstream queue remains unbounded.

- [x] Outstanding/high-water command counts exposed through engine status/diagnostics.

Not an atomic audio transaction: group commands may span callback blocks; direct jog mailbox can interleave, engine-offline/restart behavior remains unchanged. Counts include reserved sends and dequeued group tails; high water may include failed sends. They exclude the audio ring and one downstream pending command; they are not latency/acknowledgment measurements. Closing the receiver sets visible backlog to zero while retaining the historical peak. No complete queue-memory bound or overload policy claimed.
